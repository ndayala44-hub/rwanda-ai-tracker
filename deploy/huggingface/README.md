# Rwanda AI Tracker

An independent, multi-source intelligence platform on Rwanda's AI ecosystem: readiness, adoption, economic
opportunity and the evidence behind each figure.

**Copy this file to the root of your Space** — Hugging Face reads the front matter above to configure it.

Every published figure carries its source and verification state. About a third of the observations are
reported by a named source; the remainder are demo values, labelled as such throughout and filterable in
the Data Explorer. Read the About page before citing anything.

Not a government publication.
